# Pilot Buyer Outreach Template

**Status:** Draft only. This message has not been sent.

Use this for a small number of relevant B2B discovery invitations after identifying a real, public reason the recipient may own the problem. Do not scrape personal contact details, send bulk messages, or imply prior contact. Follow the [outreach policy](./OUTREACH_POLICY.md) and obtain human review before sending.

## Recipient fit

- Role/team: AI platform, agent platform, security, or procurement decision-maker
- Relevant public signal and source URL: [specific post, talk, job responsibility, or public project]
- Why this person may own agent selection or approval: [one factual sentence]
- Intended channel and business contact source: [public professional channel]
- Human reviewer and approval date: [name / date]

If the public signal or fit cannot be stated specifically, do not send.

## Message draft

**Subject:** Brief research conversation about evaluating AI agents

Hi [name],

I noticed [specific public signal and why it relates to agent selection or approval]. I’m researching how teams evaluate whether an AI agent is suitable for a particular workflow, especially what evidence reviewers need before approving one.

Would you be open to a 20-minute research conversation about how your team handled a recent agent-selection decision? This is a discovery interview, not a request to buy. I may later invite a small number of teams to consider a time-boxed paid design-partner pilot, but there is no commitment or sales obligation.

No confidential information, credentials, or customer data are needed. I’m happy to keep the conversation general, and I won’t attribute comments to you or your organization without explicit permission.

If this is outside your role, or you would prefer not to receive another note, just let me know and I won’t follow up.

Best,  
[founder name]  
Agent Underwriting Network  
[public project URL]

## Interview handling

Use the [buyer discovery interview guide](./PILOT_BUYER_INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md). Record only the minimum useful notes in a private workspace, not this public repository. Separate observed past behavior, stated interest, and willingness to pay. Treat willingness to pay as validated only when a buyer accepts a concrete pilot scope and price in writing.

## Follow-up boundary

Send at most one polite follow-up if the recipient has not opted out and the chosen channel permits it. Log opt-outs and honor them. Never include a payment link in a research invitation. Do not send the paid-pilot scope or request payment until the buyer has agreed to a specific scope and price and the seller/payment eligibility gates are resolved.
