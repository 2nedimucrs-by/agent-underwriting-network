Terms

Evidence, not warranty

Trust Cards describe evidence observed for a particular artifact, version, environment and time. They are not a warranty, guarantee, accreditation, legal certification or permission to deploy an agent.

Version-specific results

Software changes. Evidence can become stale when code, dependencies, models, configuration or execution environments change.

No pay-to-rank verdicts

Paid access or future commercial plans must not purchase a better evidence verdict. Feature access and monitoring frequency may vary by plan; evidence truth must not.

Maintainer claims

A verified maintainer claim establishes an identity or relationship to a project. It does not make the project security-verified or capability-verified.

Responsible use

Do not use the service to attack third-party systems, publish secrets, conduct unauthorized security testing, spam maintainers, or misrepresent evidence.

Availability

The public alpha may change, be incomplete or experience downtime. Users remain responsible for independent review before granting agents permissions, credentials, budgets or production access.
